About the Project

Small changes. Big environmental impact.

Waste management is the responsible collection, sorting, treatment, recycling, and safe disposal of waste. Plastic packaging, food scraps, paper, and electronic waste can harm land, water, wildlife, and human health when handled incorrectly.

ECO REVOLUTION promotes a circular approach: separate waste at its source, recover useful materials, compost suitable organic waste, and use smart tools to make collection more efficient. Our goal is to treat waste as a resource instead of simply throwing it away.

🗑️

Mixed waste is harder to recycle and can contaminate reusable materials.

Our Smart Idea

From waste collection to useful resources.

Our concept combines correct sorting, composting, recycling, and smart-bin monitoring. Organic waste can be composted; clean paper, plastic, glass, and metal can go to appropriate recycling streams. A sensor-enabled bin could alert a collection team when it is nearly full.

How It Works — Tap Each Step

Innovation & Future Scope

  • Fill-level sensors can help plan collection routes.
  • Colour-coded bins can make sorting clearer.
  • A dashboard could show bin status.
  • QR codes could share sorting guidance.
  • Future versions could track verified recycling data.
  • Community drives can encourage participation.

Prototype note: sensors and dashboards are proposed features. They require suitable hardware, testing, and installation before real-world use.

Take Action Today

Simple choices add up when we make them every day.

  • Carry a reusable bag and water bottle.
  • Separate organic waste from dry recyclables as local rules advise.
  • Compost suitable food and garden scraps safely.
  • Repair, donate, or reuse items before throwing them away.
  • Take batteries and e-waste to an authorised collection point.
